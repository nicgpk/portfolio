Agoda Growth Program

A higher commission funds ranking boost, ad spend and a Preferred Partner badge.

How it works

Typically 10–15% incremental commission (INM). The program brings higher search rank, Google and Kayak ad spend, and wider affiliate reach.

VISIBILITY

Boost Rank

Target audiences and stay dates. Pay only when a booking lands, with no lock-in.

How it works

Choose a target audience and campaign budget. Incremental commission is charged per booking, rather than per impression.

PREPAID

Agoda Advanced

Pay upfront. Agoda deducts eligible booking earnings from the balance.

How it works

AGP and Maximum Gain variants. The original source describes the prepayment as auto-renewing when the balance hits 90%.

DISTRIBUTION

Maximum Gain

Share wholesale rates across airlines, banks, loyalty apps and OTAs.

How it works

A minimum 1% discount off the standard rate, with no lock-in or extra paperwork. Distribution uses the Booking Holdings affiliate network.

PLACEMENT

Sponsored Listing

Buy a top sponsored slot in city search results, above organic results.

How it works

Choose a city, date range and slot (top 1–3), priced upfront. Available across platforms.

RATE AUTOMATION

Dynamic Rates

Automatically close the rate gap when a wholesaler undercuts.

How it works

Activate once in YCS. The existing policy allows up to 2% below the wholesaler rate, capped at 25% off.

BRANDED STOREFRONT

Flagship Store

A bespoke branded storefront for hotel chains.

How it works

A negotiated incremental commission and contract term support a dedicated store page, branded search suggestion and homepage banner.
