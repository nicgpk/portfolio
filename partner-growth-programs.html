The problem

Every program funnel optimized its own metrics. Partners struggled to discover and compare programs, while complex programs attracted less traffic.

My role

Owned design strategy end to end for Agoda’s partner growth program suite: from problem framing and stakeholder alignment to information architecture, interaction design and visual craft.

Read the complete original case study

Key choices

Design around the property, not the funnel.

A unified hub makes discovery and comparison part of one partner experience, with recommended actions and a full program catalog.

Give every program a common language.

Four co-owned tenets and seven lifecycle patterns align discovery, pre-activation confidence, activation, settings, analytics, deactivation and retention.

Align early. Scope the launch.

Weekly syncs with program owners surfaced tradeoffs. Partner research, support tickets and NPS informed the work; an impact/effort matrix shaped the MVP.
