Product Design Lead · Agoda

Partner Growth Programs

A unified platform housing multiple partner growth programs — replacing fragmented, siloed funnels with a holistic experience that partners can actually navigate.

Role
Product Design Lead
Team
Agoda
Platform
Web, Responsive
Duration
1 Quarter (MVP)
Stakeholders
Product Director, Sr. Business Director, multiple business owners

The Problem

Every program funnel was optimized for its own metrics — not for the partner experience. Partners couldn't discover programs, couldn't compare them, and complex programs saw dramatically less traffic. The result: fragmented growth and lost revenue.

Process

1

Leverage prior research

Synthesized existing partner pain points, support tickets, and NPS data to build a foundational understanding before any new research.

2

Competitive benchmarking

Analyzed how platforms like Airbnb, Uber Eats, and Shopify structure partner program discovery and onboarding.

3

Concept & design

Explored multiple IA models, landing page concepts, and program comparison layouts with rapid Figma prototypes.

4

Stakeholder alignment

Ran weekly syncs with product and business directors to surface tradeoffs early and secure buy-in across 5+ program owners.

5

User testing

Tested with 8 partners across 3 markets. Validated comprehension of program differences, CTAs, and overall navigation flow.

6

Prioritisation

Mapped features on an impact/effort matrix with engineering. Shipped MVP in 1 quarter by scoping ruthlessly.

7

Experimentation

Launched as an A/B test (4 Dec 2025 – 7 Jan 2026) measuring CTR, conversion, and incremental bookings.
