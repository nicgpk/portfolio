Make growth programs easier to compare.

Every program funnel was optimized for its own metrics — not for the partner experience. Partners couldn't discover programs, couldn't compare them, and complex programs saw dramatically less traffic.

My role

As Product Design Lead, I owned design strategy end to end — from problem framing and stakeholder alignment across 5+ business owners to information architecture, interaction design, and visual craft.

The work spanned Agoda's entire partner growth program suite: a unified hub across 8 programs that give properties visibility, smarter pricing, discounts, distribution, and prepaid commitments. Together they form a nine-figure revenue portfolio.

Clarify what each program offers.

Synthesized partner pain points, support tickets, and NPS data. Analyzed how leading platforms structure program discovery. Partnered with Agoda research team.

What we validated

Tested with 8 partners across 3 markets. Validated comprehension of program differences, CTAs, and navigation flow.

Four tenets for every program.

Explored multiple IA models and program comparison layouts. Ran weekly syncs with 5+ program owners to surface tradeoffs early.

Four co-owned tenets and seven repeatable lifecycle flows became the shared design system that lets mature and scaling programs ship consistently, instead of reinventing every funnel.

  1. Self-activate program with confidence

    Discovery, pre-activation confidence, and self-serve activation.

  2. Manage program with flexibility

    Program settings that let partners fine-tune their setup.

  3. Track performance clearly

    Program impact, comparisons, reporting, and recommendations.

  4. Deactivate program when needed

    Self-serve deactivation and a relevant counter-offer for retention.

Scope a focused first release.

Mapped features on impact/effort matrix. Shipped MVP in 1 quarter by scoping ruthlessly. A/B tested Dec 2025 – Jan 2026.

Give partners a clear next step.

A property-first hub: Maison Solara lands on recommended actions with clear outcomes, then can scan a compact program overview underneath.

The property, marketing wallet and active programs establish the context. Recommended cards retain each program’s benefits, commercial model, status and next action.

Product screens use illustrative wallet balances, predicted uplift, potential revenue, card metrics and program states. Activate and Manage open the corresponding lifecycle examples.

Keep the full lifecycle consistent.

Seven reusable patterns span discovery, activation, management, measurement, deactivation and retention.

01. Easily discoverable

Tenet: Self-activate program with confidence

A ranked catalog and recommendations make programs discoverable through clear entry points and relevance signals.

  1. EntryGrowth home + search
  2. RelevanceRanked by property fit
  3. Next stepClear Activate / Compare

02. Pre-activation confidence

Tenet: Self-activate program with confidence

Show benefits, ROI, eligibility, FAQs and comparisons before the partner commits.

  1. BenefitsWhat you get
  2. ROI & fitUplift + eligibility
  3. FAQObjections inline

03. Self-serve activation

Tenet: Self-activate program with confidence

A short path through terms, configuration, confirmation and activation states, followed by clear Manage and Track actions.

  1. TermsCommercial impact first
  2. ConfigureOnly outcome settings
  3. ConfirmReview & go live

04. Fine-tune a live program

Tenet: Manage program with flexibility

Fine-tune fencing, pause, blackout dates, versions and program controls, with an impact preview before saving.

  1. OpenFrom active card
  2. AdjustPreview impact
  3. SaveVersioned history

05. Program performance analytics

Tenet: Track performance clearly

Show impact KPIs and comparisons first, then reporting and one recommended next action.

  1. KPIsUplift & revenue
  2. CompareVs property avg
  3. ActOne recommendation

06. Self-serve deactivation

Tenet: Deactivate program when needed

Show performance, disclose the impact of leaving, capture a reason, then confirm and follow up. No dark patterns.

  1. PerformanceResults so far
  2. ImpactWhat you lose
  3. ReasonStructured why

07. Counter-offer for retention

Tenet: Deactivate program when needed

Offer a lighter alternative that fits the program and reason for leaving, while keeping deactivation available.

  1. MatchReason → alternative
  2. CompareSide-by-side tradeoff
  3. ChooseAccept or leave
