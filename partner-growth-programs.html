The Problem

Every program funnel was optimized for its own metrics — not for the partner experience. Partners couldn't discover programs, couldn't compare them, and complex programs saw dramatically less traffic. As Product Design Lead, I owned design strategy end to end — from problem framing and stakeholder alignment across 5+ business owners to information architecture, interaction design, and visual craft.

Process

Research & benchmarking

Synthesized partner pain points, support tickets, and NPS data. Analyzed how leading platforms structure program discovery. Partnered with Agoda research team

Concept & stakeholder alignment

Explored multiple IA models and program comparison layouts. Ran weekly syncs with 5+ program owners to surface tradeoffs early.

User testing & iteration

Tested with 8 partners across 3 markets. Validated comprehension of program differences, CTAs, and navigation flow.

Ruthless prioritisation & launch

Mapped features on impact/effort matrix. Shipped MVP in 1 quarter by scoping ruthlessly. A/B tested Dec 2025 – Jan 2026.

Program tenets & patterns

Four co-owned tenets and seven repeatable lifecycle flows. The shared design system that lets mature and scaling programs ship consistently, instead of reinventing every funnel.

    The Programs

    Eight programs that create value for property partners and Agoda — through visibility, smarter pricing, discounts, distribution, and prepaid commitments. Each designed as part of a cohesive experience within the unified program hub.

    Conversion
    Promotions

    Property sets a discount — Agoda adds a deal badge, boosts rank, and amplifies across email, push, and deal pages.

    $233M
    Est. revenue
    How it works

    Property chooses a discount type and depth (e.g. 15% off for app users, 20% off for 30+ day advance bookings). Agoda adds a deal label in search results, applies a ranking boost, and surfaces it in email, push, and deal pages. Four types: Mega Sale (755M+ impressions), Seasonal ($89.7M), Always-on ($29M), Targeted ($114.2M, 72% penetration).

    Marketing
    Agoda Growth Program

    Pay a higher commission — Agoda deploys the full marketing suite: ranking boost, ad spend, Preferred Partner badge.

    $131M
    Est. revenue
    How it works

    Property commits to a higher commission, typically 10–15% INM. Agoda deploys the full marketing stack: higher search rank, increased ad spend on Google & Kayak, wider affiliate reach, and a Preferred Partner badge on every result. Partners see +10% avg booking uplift and 1.8× more PPC/MSE ad spend.

    Prepaid
    Agoda Advanced

    Pay upfront — Agoda deducts from the balance per booking. AGP or Maximum Gain variant.

    $120M
    Est. revenue
    How it works

    Property makes a lump-sum prepayment, auto-renewed when balance hits 90%. Two variants: AGP variant (Preferred Partner badge, ranking boost, ad spend) or Maximum Gain variant (net rate improvement, 10,000+ affiliate distribution). Balance is consumed by net earnings of eligible bookings per booking.

    Distribution
    Maximum Gain

    Share wholesale rates — distributed to 10,000+ affiliate partners across airlines, banks, loyalty apps, and OTAs.

    $75M
    Est. revenue
    How it works

    Property sets a minimum 1% discount off their standard rate — no lock-in, no extra paperwork. Rates go live across airlines, banks, regional OTAs, loyalty apps, and super apps via the Booking Holdings affiliate network. Bookings arrive from audiences the property couldn't reach directly.

    Visibility
    Boost Rank

    Run short campaigns targeting specific audiences and stay dates — paying only when a booking lands. No lock-in.

    $67M
    Est. revenue
    How it works

    Property creates a campaign with a target audience (e.g. Singapore guests, next 30 days) and budget. When the target guest searches, the property is pushed higher in results. INM is charged per booking, not per impression — if no guest books, no cost.

    Placement
    Sponsored Listing

    Buy the top 1–3 slot in city search results — above all organic results, on every platform.

    $17M
    Est. revenue
    How it works

    Property picks a city, date range, and slot (top 1–3), priced upfront. For every matching search, the property appears in the sponsored top 3 — above all organic results, on all platforms. Best for peak dates or new market entry.

    Rate Automation
    Dynamic Rates

    Activate once — auto-adjusts the Agoda rate whenever a wholesaler undercuts, winning the booking back to direct.

    $7.5M
    Est. revenue
    How it works

    Property activates once in YCS. The system monitors the Agoda rate against wholesaler (SuperAgg) rates continuously. When a wholesaler undercuts, it closes the gap automatically — up to 2% below the wholesaler, capped at 25% off. The guest sees the better rate on Agoda and books direct.

    Branded Storefront
    Flagship Store

    Bespoke branded storefront for hotel chains — dedicated search suggestion, store page, and homepage banner.

    30×
    ROAS avg
    How it works

    BD team pitches and closes a bespoke deal — the chain agrees an INM% and contract term. Agoda builds a dedicated store page, branded search suggestion, and homepage banner. Live partners see +29% net room nights, +32% revenue, 30× ROAS, and 160k+ daily impressions.
