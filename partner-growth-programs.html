My Role

As Product Design Lead, I owned the end-to-end design strategy and execution — from problem framing and stakeholder alignment to IA, interaction design, and visual craft. I drove alignment across 5+ business owners with competing priorities, established the design vision for a unified partner growth platform, and mentored a junior designer on the team.

The Problem

Every program funnel was optimized for its own metrics — not for the partner experience. Partners couldn't discover programs, couldn't compare them, and complex programs saw dramatically less traffic. The result: fragmented growth and lost revenue.

Process

1

Leverage prior research

Synthesized existing partner pain points, support tickets, and NPS data to build a foundational understanding before any new research.

2

Competitive benchmarking

Analyzed how platforms like Airbnb, Uber Eats, and Shopify structure partner program discovery and onboarding.

3

Concept & design

Explored multiple IA models, landing page concepts, and program comparison layouts with rapid Figma prototypes.

4

Stakeholder alignment

Ran weekly syncs with product and business directors to surface tradeoffs early and secure buy-in across 5+ program owners.

5

User testing

Tested with 8 partners across 3 markets. Validated comprehension of program differences, CTAs, and navigation flow.

6

Prioritisation

Mapped features on an impact/effort matrix with engineering. Shipped MVP in 1 quarter by scoping ruthlessly.

7

Experimentation & launch

A/B test Dec 2025 – Jan 2026. Rolled out after statistically significant wins.

Solutions

Unified Program Hub

Single landing page surfacing all growth programs — replacing 5+ separate entry points.

Program Comparison

Side-by-side view of program benefits, requirements, and expected outcomes.

Clear Activation Path

Each program with a single primary CTA, eligibility indicators, and estimated time commitment.

Reflections

Unification beats optimization. Individually optimized funnels created a collectively broken experience. The big win came from thinking holistically.

Ruthless scoping is a design skill. Shipping MVP in 1 quarter meant saying no to 70% of the wishlist.

Business owners are users too. The hardest problem wasn't the UI — it was balancing 5+ stakeholder incentives.

Mentorship amplified impact. Coaching a junior designer multiplied our output and built capability that outlasted the project.
