# Dimensional graphics

Historical experiment, superseded by the interface-cover direction after user feedback. Current pages do not load these assets or `css/dimensional.css`. This record retains the asset provenance.

The user's requested 3D direction keeps the quiet horizontal gallery and replaces the eight flat program glyphs with a coordinated family of cobalt enamel objects. A larger hotel model anchors the property-centered hub. The same objects appear in the discovery interface, using existing program names and mechanics. All imagery is decorative and does not imply new features, contributions or outcomes.

## Materials and scale

The family uses broad bevels, cobalt faces, restrained brushed aluminum edges, a three-quarter camera and one soft upper-left light. The eight diagram images occupy 104 SVG units; the center property model occupies 164. This makes the silhouettes useful at phone size. Discovery icons occupy 56px, or 48px on phones. Adjacent text remains the accessible label.

Discount bars have a highlighted enamel front and a lower face. Their front widths remain exactly 100%, 85% and 76.5% in the initial example, and follow the existing calculator values. Developer nodes have graphite front, top and side faces; the coral signal follows the existing three-stage workflow. These surfaces use SVG/CSS, with no new runtime packages.

## Asset provenance and prompts

Tool mode: built-in image generation, transparent-background generation. The coupon was generated first; subsequent calls used that local image as a material and lighting reference. Each asset had its own call. No reference-site artwork was copied.

Initial coupon prompt:

> Create one premium 3D icon: a thick perforated coupon ticket with a simple percent symbol physically inset into its front face. Single centered isolated object, true transparent background. Product design portfolio artwork, sophisticated industrial object, not cartoon emoji. Orthographic three-quarter front camera, slight view of top and right edge, facing front-left. Rich cobalt blue enamel face, rounded broad bevels, restrained satin brushed aluminum edge, dark blue inset percent mark, crisp silhouette, broad white reflection from a large soft light above-left, restrained short soft shadow only directly under object. No pedestal, no background, no tile, no typography beyond the percent mark, no extra elements. Object fills about 82 percent of the square canvas, generous clear alpha margin. Designed to remain legible at 48px. The coupon is a solid physical sculpted object with visible thickness and gentle reflective material, deep cobalt not cyan.

Common prompt for each remaining asset, followed by its subject below:

> Use the attached coupon only as the precise material, camera, lighting and scale reference; generate a DIFFERENT isolated object. Premium sculpted 3D icon, rich cobalt enamel main faces, broad rounded bevels, restrained brushed aluminum edges, dark cobalt inset details. Consistent orthographic three-quarter front camera with top and right edge visible; soft broad studio key light above-left. Clearly physically thick, crisp silhouette legible at 48px, centered filling ~82% of square canvas. True transparent alpha background. No pedestal, no tile, no letters, no scene, no extra props. Tiny short soft shadow immediately under object only.

Selected cutouts were inspected, then converted to 256×256 transparent WebP at quality 0.86 using project-local Chromium canvas. This was format/size optimization, without cropping or retouching. The nine final assets total 118,634 bytes; each has four fully transparent corners. Original generation outputs remain outside the repository; the final web assets are self-contained in `images/dimensional/`.

## Motion and fallbacks

The existing bounded parent spring provides coordinated tilt rather than nine independent animation loops. Selection on the full work index adds a small lift and dotted ring, supported by the labeled controls. Explicit pause and reduced motion disable transitions. Reduced transparency retains opaque navigation and removes secondary CSS shadows. Forced colors hide raster objects and restore the existing vector symbols and property mark.

These are rendered 3D cutouts, not interactive 3D models; their camera and lighting are fixed. The decorative rate/developer geometry does not change the underlying simulations. Safari, Firefox and physical-device rendering still require review.
