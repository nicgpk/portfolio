## Motion

Graphics share a spring response to pointer position and native scroll. Connections draw on entry, rate bars reveal and react to input, and the developer signal settles at the selected stage. Text remains immediately readable. Animation frames stop at rest and when the page is hidden. Touch never needs pointer interaction. There is no scroll interception, pinned horizontal sequence or continuous animation loop.

A keyboard-accessible Pause motion control persists across routes and reloads. OS reduced motion takes precedence and disables movement while retaining controls. Reduced transparency produces opaque navigation. No-JavaScript examples remain readable with inactive controls and a static-calculation note.
