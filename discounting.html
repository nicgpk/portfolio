Senior Product Designer · Agoda

Discounting

Redesigned promotions and discounting products on a high-traffic page — reducing partner frustration, launching new discount types, and closing legacy gaps causing business loss.

Role
Senior Product Designer
Team
Agoda
Platform
Web, Responsive
Duration
2024 – Ongoing
Stakeholders
Sr. Product Owner, Multiple Sr. Business Owners

The Problem

Partners didn't understand how discounts stacked, legacy promotions were confusing, and launching new discounting products meant navigating conflicting stakeholder interests on a page with direct revenue impact. Every change had to be surgically precise.

Process

1

Competitive benchmarking

Studied how Booking.com, Expedia, and Airbnb handle discount visibility, stacking logic, and promotional surfaces.

2

Prioritisation & alignment

Ran workshops with 5+ business owners to surface conflicts early. Built a shared prioritisation framework around partner impact, not individual program KPIs.

3

User testing

Tested discount stacking explanations with 12 partners. Iterated on visual models until comprehension hit target.

4

Experimentation

Shipped behind feature flags. Measured handle time, activation rates, and complaint volume in controlled rollouts before full launch.

5

Iterative solutioning

Continuous refinement based on partner support tickets and agent feedback. Legacy features were harder to deprecate than new ones were to launch.

Reflections

High stakes, high precision. On a page with direct revenue impact, every feature launch required rigorous testing and rollback plans.

Removing > Adding. Introducing new discount types was easier than deprecating legacy ones. Sunset planning is design work too.

Trust is currency. Building relationships with business owners across conflicting interests was the real unlock.

Persistence compounds. 1.5 years of continuous small wins added up to double-digit activation gains.
