The decisions ↓

The problem

Partners could not tell how discounts stacked. Legacy promotions were confusing, and changes to this high-revenue surface involved conflicting stakeholder interests.

My role

Led design for Discounting 2.0 across one of Agoda’s highest-revenue partner surfaces, aligning product and business owners while protecting the partner experience.

Read the complete original case study ↗

Key choices

↳

Show the calculation before commitment.

The Net Rate Simulator makes sequential discounting visible on the remaining balance, so a partner can understand the resulting room rate.

↳

Explain the booking, night by night.

A reservation breakdown shows the promotion, percentage and monetary value for each night instead of forcing partners to reverse-engineer their earnings.

↳

Teach the rule at the decision point.

Connect booker conditions to eligible promotions, then show their priority and sequence. Iterate through partner testing, feature-flagged experiments and support feedback.
