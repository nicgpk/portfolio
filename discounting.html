Discounting 2.0

Senior Product Designer · Agoda

Know the rate. Before you commit.

Helping Agoda’s accommodation partners understand stacked discounts and preview their room rate before committing.

My contribution
Promotions design strategy, stakeholder alignment, interaction & visual design

Partners could not see what they would earn.

Partners didn't understand how discounts stacked, legacy promotions were confusing, and launching new discounting products meant navigating conflicting stakeholder interests on a page with direct revenue impact.

Every change to one of Agoda's highest-revenue partner surfaces had to be surgically precise.

Before
Partners had to work out how overlapping promotions affected their earnings.
The design response
A visible calculation before commitment, a per-night breakdown after booking, and an explanation of the stacking rule.

Align, test, and keep refining.

Business alignment and partner research shaped the first release. Experiments and support feedback guided the work after launch.

  1. Benchmarking & alignment

    Studied how leading platforms handle discount UX. Ran workshops with 5+ business owners to align on partner impact. Partnered with the Agoda research team.

  2. User testing

    Tested discount stacking explanations with 12 partners and refined the explanations based on their feedback.

  3. Experimentation

    Shipped behind feature flags. Measured handle time, activation rates, and complaint volume.

  4. Iterative refinement

    Continuous improvement based on support tickets and agent feedback over 1.5 years.
