# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, design leaders, and hiring teams reviewing Nicholas Gwee's product design leadership, decision-making, and measurable outcomes. Embedded product examples depict the workflows of accommodation partners managing and growing their business.

## Product Purpose

Present concise, credible case studies that make design ownership, process, product craft, and business impact easy to evaluate. Success means a reviewer can quickly understand the problem, Nicholas's contribution, the quality of the resulting experience, and the evidence behind its impact.

## Brand Personality

Precise, credible, and crafted. The portfolio should feel evidence-led and senior while retaining deliberate visual character and close attention to product detail.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS portfolio templates, interchangeable gradient-heavy landing pages, oversized rounded cards, and decorative UI chrome that does not clarify the work.

## Design Principles

- Lead with the product decision and its outcome.
- Show realistic product artifacts instead of relying on abstract claims.
- Make complex systems understandable without erasing important nuance.
- Use visual character deliberately, never at the expense of credibility.
- Keep case studies concise enough to scan and detailed enough to trust.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Maintain accessible contrast, keyboard-visible focus, semantic structure, reduced-motion support, readable type, and responsive layouts across portfolio pages and embedded product examples.
