How I work.

The principles I bring to every team, problem and handoff.

  1. Adaptive, not rigid

    I match the method to the challenge: which tools to use, how to sequence research, and when to move fast versus go deep.

  2. Championing the user

    I stay close to real users through discovery interviews, usability testing, funnel analysis, clickstreams and NPS signals.

  3. Think long, ship smart

    I keep the longer-term vision in mind while shipping what I can, weighing time, capacity, the strength of the evidence and who needs to be on board.

  4. Designing for business outcomes

    Design initiatives stay tied to clear goals: conversion, retention, revenue and operational efficiency.

  5. Building effective teams

    I hire, coach and create the conditions for designers to do their best work, so craft and impact scale beyond any one person.

  6. Augmenting, not replacing

    I use AI tools across research synthesis, critique, prototyping and production handoff.
