Process

Adaptive, not rigid

I match the methodology to the challenge: which tools to use, how to sequence research, and when to move fast versus go deep.

Championing the user

I stay close to real users through discovery interviews, usability testing, funnel analysis, clickstreams, and NPS signals.

Think long, ship smart

I keep the longer term vision in mind while shipping what can, weighing time, capacity, how solid the evidence is, and who needs to be on board.

Designing for business outcomes

Design initiatives stay tied to clear goals: conversion, retention, revenue, and operational efficiency.

Building effective teams

I hire, coach, and create conditions for designers to do their best work so craft and impact scale beyond any one person.

Augmenting, not replacing

I utilize these AI tools into research synthesis, critique, prototyping, and production handoff.
