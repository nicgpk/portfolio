How I work

I adapt the method to the challenge, staying close to users through discovery interviews, usability testing, funnel analysis, clickstreams and NPS signals.

I keep the longer-term vision in view while balancing evidence, capacity and stakeholder alignment. Design stays tied to conversion, retention, revenue and operational efficiency.

I hire, coach and build the conditions for teams to do their best work. AI tools augment research synthesis, critique, prototyping and production handoff: Cursor, Claude, GPT, DeepSeek and Figma.
