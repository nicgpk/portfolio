How I work.

The principles I bring to every team, problem and handoff.

  • Choose the right approach

    I match methods and research depth to the problem, the evidence we need and the time available.

  • Stay close to users

    I combine discovery interviews and usability tests with funnel, clickstream and NPS signals.

  • Balance vision and delivery

    I set a longer-term direction and prioritise delivery around evidence, capacity and stakeholder alignment.

  • Connect design to outcomes

    I tie design decisions to clear goals: conversion, retention, revenue and operational efficiency.

  • Build effective teams

    I hire and coach designers, creating the conditions for strong work and shared craft across the team.

  • Extend craft with AI

    I use AI tools across research synthesis, critique, prototyping and production handoff.
