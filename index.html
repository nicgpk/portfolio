Process

Adaptive, not rigid

Don't force every problem through the same framework. I match the methodology to the challenge. Choosing the right tools, sequencing the right research, and knowing when to move fast and when to go deep.

Championing the user

I build the conditions for teams to stay close to real users — not just at the start of a project, but continuously. That means establishing mixed-method research practices: discovery interviews, usability testing, funnel analysis, clickstreams, and NPS signals.

Think long, ship smart

Holding a longer-term vision while sequencing work that delivers near-term value.

Navigating tradeoffs

Making the best call given real constraints: time, engineering capacity, data confidence, and stakeholder alignment. I help teams surface tradeoffs and move forward without losing sight of quality or user needs.

Designing for business outcomes

I work closely with product and commercial stakeholders to ensure design initiatives are tied to clear goals: conversion, retention, revenue, or operational efficiency.

Sustained design culture

Quality doesn't happen from a single sprint, it compounds over time. I focus on creating the conditions where design decisions stick: clear principles, shared standards, and a team culture that values craft as a long-term investment.
