UX Designer · Agoda Engineering

Developer Portal & Deployment

Establishing design processes within devops — building intuitive tools for 1,000+ engineers, migrating from servers to private cloud, and making service creation 200× faster.

Role
UX Designer
Team
1 PO, 4–5 Engineers, 1 FE Engineer
Platform
Web
Duration
3 Quarters

The Problem

Agoda's internal developer platform was fragmented, slow, and unscored. Creating a service took 1–2 working days. Deploying to private cloud required tribal knowledge. There were no UX metrics, no design process, and devops had never worked with a designer before.

Goals

01

Improve developer experience with measurable UX metrics

02

Migrate from servers to private cloud via UI

03

Build design rhythm in a team that had never had one

Process

1

User research

Interviewed 15+ developers across teams. Mapped their end-to-end service creation journey, identifying every bottleneck and workaround.

2

Domain immersion

Learned Kubernetes, Docker, service meshes, and CI/CD pipelines from scratch. Built technical fluency to earn credibility with the engineering team.

3

Heuristic evaluation

Scored the existing platform against Nielsen's heuristics. Documented 47 usability issues across service creation, deployment, and monitoring flows.

4

Baseline metrics (SUS)

Ran a System Usability Scale survey across engineering. Established a baseline score of 61.9 to track improvement over time.

5

Prioritised solutioning

Split work into two phases: quick fixes (guides, quick links) to unblock developers immediately, then full automation with approval workflows.

6

Continuous measurement

Re-ran SUS every 2 quarters. Tracked service creation time and deployment satisfaction. Established a cadence the team continued after the project.
