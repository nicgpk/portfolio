The problem

The internal platform was fragmented and slow. A flat homepage and missing guidance made service creation depend on Slack and tribal knowledge, with no usability baseline.

My role

Led 0-to-1 design for Developer Portal and Deployment as the first designer in DevOps, building domain understanding and a measurable foundation for developer experience.

Read the complete original case study

Key choices

Organize around the task.

Replace a flat link dump with Manage, Plan, Build, Secure and Monitor. A searchable catalog brings ownership, source and compliance into view.

Unblock first. Then automate.

Initial fixes added in-context guidance, skipped unused repository steps, and supported leave and resume during AD handoffs. Later work introduced automation and approval workflows.

Make deployment a reviewable decision.

A staged private-cloud flow covers environment setup, canary rollout across data centers, and review. Information hierarchy surfaces environments and critical actions.
