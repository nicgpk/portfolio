01 of 08 — Agoda Growth Program

Marketing program

Pay more commission. Get seen everywhere.

Properties agree to a higher commission — typically 10–15%. Agoda deploys a full marketing suite in return: higher search ranking, more ad spend on Google & Kayak, wider affiliate reach, and a Preferred Partner badge guests see on every result.

$131M
2025 Revenue
14%
Penetration
+10%
Avg booking uplift
Known friction
23% of partners are confused about the value prop — unclear how AGP differs from Boost Rank or Sponsored Listing
Hotel benefits limited to ranking boost and badge — additional benefits (affiliate distribution, increased ad bid) have limited measurable impact
Deep reliance on Account Managers — 21pp fewer digital self-serve activations vs Booking.com; removal of 3-month lock-in in progress (Jun 2026)
How it works
Step 01
Property commits to a higher commission
Typically 10–15% INM. The partner agrees upfront — Agoda earns more per booking in return for more marketing effort.
Step 02
Agoda deploys the full marketing stack
Higher search rank, increased ad spend on Google & Kayak, wider affiliate reach, and a Preferred Partner badge on every result.
Step 03
More visibility drives more bookings
Partners see +10% avg booking uplift and 1.8× more PPC/MSE ad spend. The higher commission funds the marketing — both sides gain.

02 of 08 — Boost Rank

On-demand visibility

Run a campaign. Show up at the right moment.

Unlike AGP, Boost Rank is on-demand. Properties create short campaigns targeting specific audiences and stay dates — paying only when a booking lands. No lock-in. Primarily activated by SAMs and Account Teams.

$67M
2025 Revenue
16%
Penetration
No
Lock-in period
Known friction
Name "AGX" is non-descriptive — competitors use "Traffic Boost" (Trip.com) and "Visibility Booster" (Booking.com); NAM rebranding to Boost Rank already drove growth
Partners don't understand what a boost rank number means (e.g. rank 1 vs 2 vs 5 — what is the actual impact?)
AGX campaigns were (mis)used to charge INM for unrelated programs (Flagship, Mega Sale) — scalable INM charging infrastructure being built to fix this
How it works
Step 01
Property creates a campaign
Sets a target audience (e.g. Singapore guests, next 30 days) and a budget. No lock-in — campaigns can be paused anytime.
Step 02
Agoda boosts rank for matching searches
When the target guest searches, the property is pushed higher in results — ahead of properties with no active campaign.
Step 03
Pay only when a booking lands
INM is charged per booking, not per impression. If no guest books, no cost. Campaign spend scales directly with results.

03 of 08 — Sponsored Listing

Guaranteed placement

Buy the slot. Own the top of the page.

Properties pay to be pinned in the top 3 spots in their city on Agoda search results — for any check-in date range they choose. No ranking algorithm. They buy the placement outright across desktop, mobile web, and app.

$17M
2025 Revenue
2%
Penetration
Top 3
Guaranteed placement
Known friction
2% penetration despite 6+ years live — high cost of guaranteed placement limits it to peak-season, specific-date use cases
ROI is hard to measure for partners — they pay upfront for a slot but attributing incremental bookings from paid vs organic placement is unclear
Competes with BHSL (Booking Holdings Sponsored Listings) — overlapping value props create internal confusion about which to pitch
How it works
Step 01
Property buys a placement slot
They pick a city, date range, and slot (top 1–3). Priced upfront — independent of organic rank or review score.
Step 02
Pinned at the top, every search
For every search matching that city and date, the property appears in the sponsored top 3 — above all organic results, on all platforms.
Step 03
Impressions convert to bookings
A Sponsored badge signals prominence to guests. Best used for peak dates or new market entry — where being first matters most.

04 of 08 — Promotions

Conversion

Property sets a discount. Agoda amplifies it everywhere.

Properties offer a discount — Agoda adds a deal badge, boosts ranking, and surfaces it in email and push. Four types cover different booking patterns, from all-time record Mega Sale events to always-on daily deals.

$233M
2025 Combined
72%
Max penetration
#1
Largest by revenue
Known friction
Discount stacking is opaque — partners can't simulate how promotions stack before activating, leading to accidental over-discounting
Partners struggle to decide which promotion type suits their business — 4 types with overlapping use cases and no clear guidance on which to pick
Targeted Promotions (Rate Channels) at 72% penetration but 14% avg effective discount — recategorisation underway to improve stacking and effective discount
How it works
Step 01
Property sets a discount
They choose a discount type and depth — e.g. 15% off for app users, or 20% off for stays booked 30+ days ahead.
Step 02
Agoda adds a deal badge and boosts rank
The discounted rate gets a deal label in search results, a ranking boost, and surfaces in email, push, and deal pages.
Step 03
Four types — one for every pattern
Mega Sale (755M+ impressions), Seasonal ($89.7M), Always-on ($29M), Targeted ($114.2M, 72% penetration).

05 of 08 — Agoda Dynamic Rates (ADR+)

Rate automation · Launched 2025

Rates adjusted automatically. No effort needed.

Property activates once. When a wholesaler undercuts Agoda's direct rate, ADR+ closes the gap automatically.

$7.5M
2025 · scaling
11×
Growth at launch
7%
Penetration
Known friction
Low awareness — partners don't know ADR+ exists or how it works; discovery relies almost entirely on SAM pitch with no self-serve entry point at launch
Churn: hotels deactivate when they notice rate was adjusted without understanding it was to win back a booking lost to a wholesaler
Eligibility limited — requires ≥5% SuperAgg share and excludes Key Accounts and Direct Connect hotels, capping addressable pool
How it works
Step 01
Property activates once in YCS
No ongoing management needed. ADR+ monitors the property's Agoda rate against wholesaler (SuperAgg) rates continuously.
Step 02
Gap detected — rate auto-adjusted
When a wholesaler undercuts Agoda's direct rate, ADR+ closes the gap automatically — up to 2% below the wholesaler, capped at 25% off.
Step 03
Booking stays on Agoda direct
The guest sees the better rate on Agoda and books direct. The property earns higher net revenue — no intermediary cut.

06 of 08 — Maximum Gain

Distribution · formerly Beds Network

Share your rates. Reach guests everywhere.

Properties distribute their rates across Booking Holdings' global affiliate network. Rooms appear on 10,000+ partner platforms — airlines, loyalty apps, regional OTAs, financial services — without any extra work from the property.

$75M
2025 Revenue
30%
Penetration
10k+
Affiliate partners
Known friction
"Beds Network" brand created confusion with wholesale — partners and BDMs conflated it with B2B rate distribution; rebranded to Maximum Gain in Mar 2026
Not supported in North America — NAM has its own Private B2B rate channel; Maximum Gain appearing in YCS for NAM partners caused confusion post-rebrand
Approaching penetration limits in some markets — selective high-penetration markets have limited room to grow further
How it works
Step 01
Property shares their wholesale rates
They set a minimum 1% discount off their standard rate. No lock-in, no extra paperwork — managed entirely in YCS.
Step 02
Agoda distributes to 10,000+ partners
Rates go live across airlines, banks, regional OTAs, loyalty apps, and super apps — the full Booking Holdings affiliate network.
Step 03
New guests, no extra effort
Bookings arrive from audiences the property couldn't reach directly. Best for hotels with low occupancy who want to fill rooms fast.

Your brand. Your storefront. On Agoda.

A premium branded storefront for hotel chains and key accounts pitched by BD teams. Unlike the other six programs, this is not self-serve — it's a bespoke partnership. Chains get a fully customisable store page, branded search, homepage banner, and direct property links.

08 of 08 — Agoda Advanced

Prepaid · two variants: commission or net rate

Pay upfront. Lock in better terms immediately.

Property makes a lump-sum prepayment to Agoda — Agoda deducts from the balance as eligible bookings come in. Two variants: AGP Advanced (higher commission/INM, same benefits as AGP) or Maximum Gain Advanced (net rate improvement, same distribution as Maximum Gain). One upfront commitment, one program type.

$120M
Paid exposure Q4'25
10%
Penetration
$280M
Target Dec'27
Known friction
Complex dual-variant structure (commission vs net rate) — partners and BDs struggle to choose the right variant; ~3% of activations are self-serve today vs 50% target
Approaching penetration limits in selected markets — expansion to JP, MEA, and long-tail hotels underway but requires new eligibility logic and risk monitoring
Cash commitment is a barrier — upfront prepayment deters smaller, cash-constrained properties even when the ROI is positive
How it works
Step 01
Property makes an upfront prepayment
A lump-sum paid to Agoda — auto-renewed when balance hits 90%. Two options: higher commission (INM) or improved net rate (Beds-style).
Step 02
Agoda delivers the benefits of the chosen variant
AGP variant: Preferred Partner badge, ranking boost, increased ad spend. Maximum Gain variant: net rate improvement, 10,000+ affiliate distribution.
Step 03
Balance deducted per booking, not upfront
Prepayment balance is consumed by net earnings of eligible bookings. Property improves cash flow, Agoda secures committed spend — both sides lock in.
