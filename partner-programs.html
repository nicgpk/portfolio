The Problem

Every program funnel was optimized for its own metrics — not for the partner experience. Partners couldn’t discover programs, couldn’t compare them, and complex programs saw dramatically less traffic. As Product Design Lead, I owned the end-to-end design strategy — from problem framing and stakeholder alignment across 5+ business owners to IA, interaction design, and visual craft.

Process

1

Research & benchmarking

Synthesized partner pain points, support tickets, and NPS data. Analyzed how leading platforms structure program discovery. Partnered with Agoda research team

2

Concept & stakeholder alignment

Explored multiple IA models and program comparison layouts. Ran weekly syncs with 5+ program owners to surface tradeoffs early.

3

User testing & iteration

Tested with 8 partners across 3 markets. Validated comprehension of program differences, CTAs, and navigation flow.

4

Ruthless prioritisation & launch

Mapped features on impact/effort matrix. Shipped MVP in 1 quarter by scoping ruthlessly. A/B tested Dec 2025 – Jan 2026.

Solutions

Unified Program Hub

Single landing page surfacing all growth programs — replacing 5+ separate entry points.

Program Comparison

Side-by-side view of program benefits, requirements, and expected outcomes.

Clear Activation Path

Each program with a single primary CTA, eligibility indicators, and estimated time commitment.

Results

A unified program hub grounded in co-owned tenets and a shared lifecycle design pattern.

01 of 08 — Agoda Growth Program

Marketing program

Pay more commission. Get seen everywhere.

Properties agree to a higher commission — typically 10–15%. Agoda deploys a full marketing suite in return: higher search ranking, more ad spend on Google & Kayak, wider affiliate reach, and a Preferred Partner badge guests see on every result.

$131M
2025 Revenue
14%
Penetration
+10%
Avg booking uplift
Design challenge
23% of partners confused about value prop — unclear how AGP differs from other programs like Boost Rank or Sponsored Listing
Deep reliance on Account Managers — 21pp fewer digital self-serve activations vs Booking.com
How it works
Step 01
Property commits to a higher commission
Typically 10–15% INM. The partner agrees upfront — Agoda earns more per booking in return for more marketing effort.
Step 02
Agoda deploys the full marketing stack
Higher search rank, increased ad spend on Google & Kayak, wider affiliate reach, and a Preferred Partner badge on every result.
Step 03
More visibility drives more bookings
Partners see +10% avg booking uplift and 1.8× more PPC/MSE ad spend.

02 of 08 — Boost Rank

On-demand visibility

Run a campaign. Show up at the right moment.

Unlike AGP, Boost Rank is on-demand. Properties create short campaigns targeting specific audiences and stay dates — paying only when a booking lands. No lock-in. Primarily activated by SAMs and Account Teams.

$67M
2025 Revenue
16%
Penetration
No
Lock-in period
Design challenge
Partners don't understand what a boost rank number means (e.g. rank 1 vs 2 vs 5 — what is the actual impact on bookings?)
How it works
Step 01
Property creates a campaign
Sets a target audience (e.g. Singapore guests, next 30 days) and a budget. No lock-in — campaigns can be paused anytime.
Step 02
Agoda boosts rank for matching searches
When the target guest searches, the property is pushed higher in results — ahead of properties with no active campaign.
Step 03
Pay only when a booking lands
INM is charged per booking, not per impression. If no guest books, no cost.

05 of 08 — Agoda Dynamic Rates (ADR+)

Rate automation · Launched 2025

Rates adjusted automatically. No effort needed.

Property activates once. When a wholesaler undercuts Agoda's direct rate, ADR+ closes the gap automatically.

$7.5M
2025 · scaling
11×
Growth at launch
7%
Penetration
Design challenge
Churn: hotels deactivate when they notice the rate was adjusted without understanding it was to win back a booking lost to a wholesaler
How it works
Step 01
Property activates once in YCS
No ongoing management needed. ADR+ monitors the property's Agoda rate against wholesaler (SuperAgg) rates continuously.
Step 02
Gap detected — rate auto-adjusted
When a wholesaler undercuts Agoda's direct rate, ADR+ closes the gap automatically — up to 2% below the wholesaler, capped at 25% off.
Step 03
Booking stays on Agoda direct
The guest sees the better rate on Agoda and books direct. The property earns higher net revenue — no intermediary cut.

08 of 08 — Agoda Advanced

Prepaid · two variants

Pay upfront. Lock in better terms immediately.

Property makes a lump-sum prepayment to Agoda — Agoda deducts from the balance as eligible bookings come in. Two variants: AGP Advanced (higher commission/INM) or Maximum Gain Advanced (net rate improvement, same distribution).

$120M
Paid exposure Q4'25
10%
Penetration
$280M
Target Dec'27
Design challenge
Complex dual-variant structure (commission vs net rate) — partners and BDs struggle to choose the right variant; ~3% of activations are self-serve vs 50% target
How it works
Step 01
Property makes an upfront prepayment
A lump-sum paid to Agoda — auto-renewed when balance hits 90%. Two options: higher commission (INM) or improved net rate.
Step 02
Agoda delivers the benefits of the chosen variant
AGP variant: Preferred Partner badge, ranking boost, increased ad spend. Maximum Gain variant: net rate improvement, 10,000+ affiliate distribution.
Step 03
Balance deducted per booking, not upfront
Prepayment balance is consumed by net earnings of eligible bookings. Both sides lock in committed spend.

Reflections

Ruthless scoping is a design skill

Shipping MVP in 1 quarter meant saying no to 70% of the wishlist. Scope discipline created space for quality.

Business owners are users too

The hardest problem wasn't the UI — it was balancing 5+ stakeholder incentives. Alignment was the real design work.

A $521M revenue line built on mutual value

Every dollar comes from a property that got something real back: more bookings, better visibility, more distribution.

Mentorship amplified impact

Coaching a junior designer multiplied our output and built capability that outlasted the project.

What I'd do differently

Validate the IA with a card sort before visual design. We got it right, but it took more iteration than necessary.
